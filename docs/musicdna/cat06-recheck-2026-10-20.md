The full draft reports four movement tests; three of those are held with inactive songs. The only active movement test is **De Selby (Part 2) vs Breezeblocks**, with a 25-point gap.

`claims.ts` requires at least three distinct supporting pairings. A listener restricted to the currently available Alternative evidence cannot reach a supported movement claim, even by agreeing with every available movement test. Direct execution of `buildLedger` confirmed one movement support and `eligible: false`. Scale can reach three supports only if all three available tests agree; it has no room for a counterexample at that threshold.

Five tests per axis is a coverage target, not a guarantee that a short session will encounter enough supports. Add useful low-pole material as part of this work: the active CAT06 subset has minimum movement 48, scale 45 and community 40. It remains concentrated away from stillness, intimacy and solitude. Do not change a song's scores merely to manufacture these anchors.
